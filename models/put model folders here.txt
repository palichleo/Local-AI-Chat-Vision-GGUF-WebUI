Put MODEL FOLDERS here, not loose GGUF files.

models/
  Your model folder name/
    model.gguf
    mmproj.gguf

The folder name is the model name shown in Atelier local.
Use one main GGUF and optionally its matching vision projector.
The projector filename must contain "mmproj" (case insensitive).
Open Configuration to detect folders, choose one, then click "Ajouter un LLM".
GGUF weights are ignored by Git. Keep llama.cpp outside these model folders.
